# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A 2027 computer science graduate seeking US software engineering and related entry-level roles, open to all US locations and work arrangements. The product preserves work-authorization uncertainty and supports searches requiring future employer sponsorship.

## Product Purpose

Save the time spent searching multiple job sources. Find many relevant openings, preserve direct application links, and let the user decide whether their skills or interests match. Never use resume matching to hide jobs.

## Brand Personality

Simple, reliable, trustworthy. The user explicitly requested a minimal interface with no fluff and no notifications.

## Anti-references

Decorative dashboards, promotional content, match scores, unnecessary onboarding, automatic applications, and silent filtering based on the resume.

## Design Principles

- Applying is the primary action.
- Separate evidence from assumptions.
- Preserve uncertain openings for review.
- Make source failures and stale data visible.
- Keep collection working without AI access.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Implementation defaults: semantic forms and tables, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, text labels for all states, and responsive layouts. No user-specific accommodations were requested.
