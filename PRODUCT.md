# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ria, a Drexel BS in Business and Engineering student, Finance minor, graduating June 2027. She targets business analysis, financial analysis/FP&A, data analysis, strategy, consulting and related business/finance graduate roles. Her SWE concentration does not make software engineering a target. All industries, pay levels and work arrangements in the United States, Canada and United Kingdom are acceptable. US roles must preserve OPT and future-sponsorship uncertainty; Canada/UK authorization is informational.

## Product Purpose

Save the time spent searching multiple job sources. Publish one fixed daily edition at 6:30 PM Eastern, with up to 100 recently published employer postings that satisfy US/Canada/UK location, 0–3 years of required experience, and country-specific work-authorization rules. Allow clear entry-level postings with unstated start dates, label uncertainty, preserve application links, and let the user decide whether their skills or interests match. Never use resume matching to hide jobs.

## Brand Personality

Simple, reliable, trustworthy. The user explicitly requested a minimal interface with no fluff and no notifications.

## Anti-references

Decorative dashboards, promotional content, match scores, unnecessary onboarding, automatic applications, and silent filtering based on the resume.

## Design Principles

- Applying is the primary action.
- Separate evidence from assumptions.
- Hold unclear dates and experience evidence visible with uncertainty labels. Missing publication dates remain in Discoveries. Never pad an edition to reach 100.
- Applied and skipped jobs leave the edition immediately, without replacement. Published membership stays fixed; newly discovered disqualifying evidence can withdraw a job.
- Never relabel discovery or update time as original publication time.
- Make source failures and stale data visible.
- Keep collection working without AI access. Use only Ria’s separately connected ChatGPT account for optional advice.
- Never hide jobs based on resume skills, GPA, university prestige, employer industry or compensation.
- Require positive US, Canada or UK location evidence in the main views; hold ambiguous geography in audit views.
- Keep application filters separate from source administration and retain personal history when a posting no longer qualifies.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Implementation defaults: semantic forms and tables, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, text labels for all states, and responsive layouts. No user-specific accommodations were requested.
